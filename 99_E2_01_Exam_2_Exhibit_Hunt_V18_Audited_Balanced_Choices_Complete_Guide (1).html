Exam 2 — Exhibit Hunt

Four original business cases · Two practice versions · evidence-based decisions

Your mission: work as the analyst for four original business clients. Read a client briefing, identify the right statistical exhibit from a deliberately mixed appendix, interpret evidence carefully, and make a defensible business recommendation.
01 · Read the caseIdentify the outcome, explanatory variable(s), sampling units and management question.
02 · Match the outputOpen the floating Statistical Appendix. A variance F test, two versions of an independent-samples t-test, ANOVA with Tukey, and regression results are mixed together.
03 · Explain the statisticsInspect group summaries, test statistics, p-values, confidence intervals, model slopes and assumptions. Use the Shadow Guide for direct scenario clues and highlighted output cells.
04 · Advise the companySeparate statistical significance from practical value and distinguish associations from causal conclusions.
