The six-row table hinted at a pattern. The scatterplot lets you see all observations at once, including how tightly or loosely they follow that pattern.

New idea: a trend line

A single line can summarize the center of a roughly linear pattern. It will not hit every point—and it is not supposed to.

We have still not named the statistical method.

What do we want a better model to do?

Plain-English goal

We want to ask:

“Among otherwise similar pop-ups, what changes with mascot height?”

This is the leap

A single trend line asks about one predictor. A richer model can keep several predictors in the conversation at the same time.

You are now asking for a model that can use several predictors at once—before seeing its equation or its formal name.

“Holding other things constant” starts as a business question.
9:18 AM · A data problem

How can a model use a predictor that says “Mall / Campus / Festival”?

Traffic is numerical. Mascot height is numerical. Venue type is categorical. The computer needs a consistent way to represent those categories.

Campus 1

Campus 0
Festival 1
Mall is the baseline. Two on/off indicators are enough to distinguish three categories. Later you will call these dummy variables or indicator variables. You usually will not create them by hand in Jamovi—the software handles the coding when a predictor is entered as a factor.

Why not create three indicators—Mall, Campus, and Festival—along with an intercept?

What a category coefficient will eventually mean

If Mall is the reference, a Campus coefficient answers:

How much higher or lower is predicted revenue for Campus than Mall, after accounting for the other predictors?

Notice what you have built

A model can now combine:

• quantitative predictors
• categorical predictors
• one quantitative outcome

Categories can enter the same model through indicator coding.
The reveal

You have been asking for regression.

Regression is a way to describe and predict a quantitative outcome from one or more predictors. You already discovered its core ideas before seeing the formal notation.

What you just did

Simple linear regression

One quantitative predictor helped summarize and predict revenue with a line.

Next: equation, slope, intercept, residuals, uncertainty, inference.

Why the first story broke

Multiple regression

Several predictors can enter together so each coefficient answers a conditional question: what changes when the others are held constant?

Next: adjusted coefficients, model fit, prediction, diagnostics.

How words entered

Dummy variables

Categorical predictors can be represented with indicator variables relative to a reference group.

11:53 AM · The payoff

What did multiple regression actually change?

The first model used only mascot height. The richer model used mascot height + foot traffic + venue type at the same time. That lets us ask what changes with one predictor while the others are held roughly comparable.

Height by itself+$1.41k

Each extra foot of mascot height was associated with about $1.41k more weekend revenue when height was the only predictor.

Height after adjustment−$0.24k

Once traffic and venue were included, the remaining height association became small and slightly negative. The evidence was not strong enough to separate it clearly from no remaining association.

Foot traffic+$1.22k

About $1.22k more predicted revenue for each additional 100 visitors, comparing pop-ups with the same height and venue type.

Venue vs. Mall+$8.2k / +$13.4k

Campus and Festival pop-ups were predicted higher than Mall pop-ups after accounting for traffic and mascot height.

The business story changed. Taller Bigfoots looked powerful when viewed alone. Once the model compared more similar pop-ups, most of that apparent advantage was tied to where the event happened and how many people showed up, not to mascot height itself. The richer model described about 92% of the observed revenue variation, compared with about 72% for height alone.

What to notice

What multiple regression can do

• Forecast a quantitative outcome using several predictors at once.
• Compare one predictor after accounting for others.
• Include categories such as venue type through dummy variables.
• Show whether a simple one-variable story changes after context is added.

What it cannot prove here

This is still observational data. The model can improve description and forecasting, but it does not prove that changing mascot height will change revenue.

That is why the best business decision is still a controlled pilot before a $1.28M rollout.
