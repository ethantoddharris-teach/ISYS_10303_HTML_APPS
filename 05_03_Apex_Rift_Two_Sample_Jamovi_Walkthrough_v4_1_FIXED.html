Apex Rift rollout decision
Apex Rift operates high-end indoor electric-kart arenas. Its new GhostLine system projects an augmented-reality racing line into a driver visor, cueing braking points, apex timing, and throttle release. The system is designed for first-time guests, who often lose speed because they brake too early or enter corners inconsistently. A full retrofit costs about $165,000 per venue and adds calibration time between heats. In a controlled pilot, first-time guests were randomly assigned to either GhostLine or the current Standard guidance. Each guest drove only one format. Operations recorded Laps_Per_Hour. Leadership will consider a full rollout only if the population mean gain is convincingly positive; the finance team says the technology becomes operationally worthwhile at about +1.0 lap per guest-hour.
