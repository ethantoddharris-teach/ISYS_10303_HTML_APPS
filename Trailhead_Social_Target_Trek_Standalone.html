TRAILHEAD SOCIAL • VENUE EXPANSION STUDY
Target Trek: Is Adventure worth the upgrade?

Trailhead Social is a new casual-sports venue built around Target Trek, a nine-station course where guests move through recreational archery challenges involving accuracy, team play, moving targets, and risk-reward scoring.

The venue currently experiments with six course formats: Adventure, Classic, Competitive, Glow, Family, and Speed. Management has noticed that Adventure guests may spend more on food, drinks, and add-on challenges than Classic guests, but Adventure also requires more elaborate targets, additional reset time, and greater staffing.

Leadership wants to know whether Adventure guests have higher mean spending per guest than Classic guests and, more importantly, whether the advantage is large enough to justify converting additional Classic courses to Adventure.
